# 4.3 Tracing Runs with MLflow

In this lesson we start MLflow locally, trace a media-generation run, and inspect the generated assets, judge verdicts, timing, cost and failures for each record. Then we compute success rate, p95 time per asset and cost per accepted asset from the traces. You're done when all three numbers come from your traces. On a 10-record run, p95 is close to the slowest record, so repeat this on your full run for the capstone.
